# Analytics and Footage

Look back at what a system recorded, and review the photos and clips it captured.

## Analytics

Go to **Overview > Analytics** to see totals, charts and logs for any date range.

![Analytics page](/img/software/iriv-edgeai-studio/image138.webp)

The Analytics page consists of several sections that you should be familiar with. Its cards follow whatever your Workflow tracks, so other templates show different cards.

1. **Page Buttons**

    - **Date range** - sets the period shown (e.g. 1 Oct 2026 - 31 Oct 2026).

    - **CSV** - downloads the data for the selected period.

    - **Edit layout** - opens the Analytics Builder to change the page.

    - **Footage** - jumps to the Footage page.

    ![Date range, CSV, Edit layout and Footage buttons](/img/software/iriv-edgeai-studio/image134.webp)

2. **Calendar Strip** - Click a day to show only that day; Today is highlighted. Use the arrows to move between days.

3. **Summary Cards** - Totals for the selected period, e.g. the current count and total people detected.

4. **Charts** - Show how the values changed over time, e.g. people detected per hour or per week.

5. **Log Table** - Every recorded entry with its time and values, e.g. When, object id and people.

### Customise the Analytics page

Click **Edit layout** to open the Analytics Builder. It looks like the Dashboard Builder, with an extra time range so you can preview the charts with real data.

![Analytics Builder](/img/software/iriv-edgeai-studio/image54.webp)

1. **Widgets**

    - **Image card** - a picture you upload.

    - **Text card** - your own words.

    - Drag a widget onto the canvas, or double-click it to add it.

2. **Dashboard cards**

    - The cards you drew on the Workflow canvas, grouped by type (Number tile, Line chart, Area chart).

    - Drag one onto the canvas, or click it to add it.

3. **Size and View**

    - **Small / Medium / Big** - the size of the next widget you add.

    - **View** - preview the finished Analytics page.

4. **Canvas**

    - Move a widget with its top-left handle and resize it with the bottom-right corner.

    - Use the arrows to move it up or down, or **×** to remove it.

    - Cards show **AI OFFLINE - Detection is off** until the system is running.

5. **Properties**

    - Click a widget on the canvas to edit its settings here.

6. **Time range, Save and Back**

    - **Today / Week / Month** - the time range the charts show while you design the page.

    - **Save** - saves the layout; a “saved” label appears.

    - **Back to Analytics** - returns to the Analytics page.

## Footage

Go to **Overview > Footage** to view, download or export the photos and clips the system has captured (e.g. by a Snapshot or Record Clip node). **Snap** and **Record** must be switched on in [System Control](./run-and-dashboard.md#system-control).

![Footage page](/img/software/iriv-edgeai-studio/image35.webp)

1. **Date Range and Events Logs**

    - **Date range** - chooses the period to show.

    - **Events Logs** - jumps to the Events Logs page to see the events behind the captures.

2. **Photos / Clips**

    - **Photos / Clips** - switch between captured photos and video clips; the number in brackets shows how many there are.

    - **Select all** - selects every item on the page.

3. **View and Actions**

    - **Grid / List** - choose how the items are laid out.

    - **Download all** - downloads the items.

    - **Export evidence** - exports the items as evidence.

    - **Delete** - removes the selected items.

    ![Grid/List, Download all, Export evidence and Delete](/img/software/iriv-edgeai-studio/image83.webp)

4. **Captured Items** - Each card shows the picture, its tag (e.g. capture) and the time it was taken; tick its box to select it.

:::note

If nothing has been captured yet, the page shows “No images captured” with a Set up capture button.

:::
