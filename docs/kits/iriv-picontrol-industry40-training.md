# IRIV PiControl Industry 4.0 Training Kit & Workshop

<img src="https://static.cytron.io/image/cache/catalog/products/V-KIT-IRIV/iriv-training-kit-new-main-800x800.png" alt="IRIV PiControl Industry 4.0 Training Kit" width="360" loading="lazy" />

**Product code:** V-KIT-IRIV (training kit only: CK-KIT-IRIVPCT) · [Product page](https://my.cytron.io/cytron-industry/p-iriv-picontrol-industry-4p0-kit-and-workshops)

A DIN-rail training panel built around the IRIV PiControl, sold on its own or with a two-day hands-on workshop run by Cytron, the designer and manufacturer of IRIV PiControl. Participants take the kit home after the workshop.

## Training Kit Contents

| Qty | Item |
|---|---|
| 1 | [IRIV PiControl](../products/iriv-picontrol/index.md) **CM4 Wireless, 2GB RAM / 16GB eMMC** (CR2032 fitted; no NVMe SSD or mini PCIe card) |
| 1 | Meanwell DIN-rail power supply, 24V 24W |
| 1 | Illuminated push button (used as digital input and output) |
| 1 | Industrial RS485 temperature and humidity sensor |
| 1 | ADL200 single-phase AC power meter, RS485 Modbus RTU |
| 1 | USB-C cable |
| 1 | DIN-rail rack / enclosure, pre-assembled (some wiring may be left for the workshop) |

The sensor and power meter share the RS485 bus (`/dev/ttyACM0`), so they need different Modbus addresses.

## Workshop

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Duration | 2 days, 9.00 am – 5.00 pm |
| Where | Cytron HQ, Bukit Mertajam, Penang (max 5 participants), or at your company (min 3 participants) |
| Included | Training kit to keep, workshop materials, meals and tea breaks, participation certificate, Cytron factory tour (HQ) |
| HRDC | Cytron is an HRD Corp certified training provider; the course is HRDC claimable — see [How to claim HRDC](https://my.cytron.io/tutorial/how-to-claim-hrdc-training) |
| Bring | Your own laptop, Windows or macOS |
| For | Lecturers, trainers, system integrators, engineers, researchers and companies evaluating IRIV PiControl |

**Outline:** introduction to IR4.0 and IRIV PiControl · hardware specification · getting started with IRIV PiControl and Raspberry Pi CM4 · Node-RED and its dashboard · reading the RS485 temperature and humidity sensor · reading the single-phase power meter · MQTT · data gathering and database · Blynk and ThingSpeak IoT platforms · basic Python · Linux command line.

Dates and seat prices change — check the product page, or request a session.
